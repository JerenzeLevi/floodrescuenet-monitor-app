System status · Live monitoring

When the water rises, the net is already moving.

FloodRescueNet Monitor is the Android companion for an ESP32-based automated flood response system — ultrasonic water-level sensing, a motorized rescue-net barrier, and a live dashboard built for barangay disaster response teams.

What it is

Monitoring that finally acts.

Most flood systems only detect and alert. This one closes the loop: the moment the water sensor crosses the danger threshold, the ESP32 drives a motorized net across the channel so anyone swept by the current has something to hold. FloodRescueNet Monitor is the app that lets a response team watch that happen in real time — and step in manually when they need to.

Purpose-built for responders

Designed for barangay officials and disaster response teams — not the general public. Access is gated to verified accounts per barangay.

Live, not delayed

The ESP32 streams distance readings and system state to Firebase; the app reflects Normal, Warning, or Danger the instant it changes.

Fail-safe by design

Auto-deploy on danger, auto-retract once the level is safe, and a manual override for the team on the ground.

Inside the app

Everything the response team needs on one screen.

Dashboard

Current water level, live status tier, net position (stored / deployed), and connection health at a glance.

Live Monitoring

A continuously updating readout of sensor distance and threshold state, so the trend is visible before it becomes an emergency.

Alerts & History

A timestamped log of every Warning and Danger event and every net deployment, for review and reporting after the flood.

Manual Deploy

An authenticated control to deploy or retract the net on demand when a responder judges the situation faster than the sensor.

Recent Deployments

The last actuations of the barrier — what triggered them, when, and how long the net stayed out.

Emergency Contacts

Quick-dial numbers for the local rescue chain, kept with the barangay's own configuration.

The loop

Detect → Decide → Deploy → Retract.

A four-step cycle that runs on the hardware itself. The app observes it and can intervene, but the rescue action does not depend on a phone being connected.

01

Detect

An HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor reads the distance to the water surface many times a second.

02

Decide

The ESP32 maps that distance to Normal, Warning, or Danger against calibrated thresholds.

03

Deploy

On Danger, the L298N-driven motor winds the net across the channel and the buzzer sounds.

04

Retract

Once the level holds below Warning for five seconds, the net is drawn back and the channel reopens.
