1. Who we are

FloodRescueNet Monitor ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is an Android application developed by Soul Grievers, a student research team at Saint Columban College, College of Computing Studies, Pagadian City, Philippines. The app is the companion to an ESP32-based automated flood response system built as an undergraduate thesis project.

For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at omandamjerenze@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the FloodRescueNet Monitor Android app, the optional mobile-linked 3D website prototype, and their Firebase backend. It does not cover any third-party website or service that is not operated by us, even if the app links to it.

The app is intended for authorised barangay officials and disaster response personnel. It is not a consumer safety product and is not intended for use by the general public.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide when you create an account

  • Email address — used as your login identifier and for account-related communication.
  • Password — handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication. It is stored by Google in hashed form; we never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Display name — the name shown for your account inside the app.
  • Barangay or organisation name — used to associate your account with the correct monitoring site and to keep each barangay's data separate.

3.2 Information created while you use the app

  • Account status — a "verified" flag that an administrator sets before your login is allowed, and basic timestamps (account created, last sign-in).
  • Manual deployment actions — when you use the in-app control to deploy or retract the net, the action, the account that triggered it, and the time are recorded.

3.3 System and sensor data (not personal data)

  • Water-level readings — the distance measured by the ultrasonic sensor at the monitoring site.
  • System state — the current alert tier (Normal, Warning, Danger) and net position (stored or deployed).
  • Event history — timestamped Warning/Danger events and net deployment/retraction events.

This data describes a river channel and a piece of hardware, not you. It is displayed in the app so a response team can monitor the site.

3.4 Website prototype connection

When you choose Connect prototype, the website sends your email and password to Firebase Authentication. The password field is cleared after sign-in; this website uses in-memory authentication rather than saving a session in browser storage. While linked, the website exchanges simulated readings, deployment commands, results and event history with the session for your approved account. Battery, rain, temperature and alignment inputs on the website are simulation values. The website does not establish that a physical sensor or motor is connected.

3.5 Information collected automatically by our service provider

Our backend runs on Google Firebase. When the app connects, Google may process standard technical information such as your device's IP address, device and operating-system type, and diagnostic or crash information, in accordance with Google's own privacy terms. We do not add any advertising, marketing, or third-party analytics SDKs to the app.

We do not collect: your phone's location, your contacts, your photos or files, your microphone or camera input, advertising identifiers, or any biometric data.

4. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and to sign you in.
  • To restrict access to verified personnel and to keep each barangay's monitoring data separate from every other barangay's.
  • To show you live water-level, alert status, and net-deployment information for your site.
  • To keep a history of alerts and deployments for after-action review and reporting.
  • To carry out a manual deploy or retract command that you issue, and to record who issued it.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical problems, misuse, or security issues.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Why we are allowed to use it

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (given when you create an account); the necessity of processing to provide a service you asked for; and our legitimate interest in keeping the system secure and functioning. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 10).

6. How information is shared

We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) processes and stores account and system data on our behalf so the app can function. Google acts as our data processor and is bound by its own security and privacy commitments.
  • Within your barangay. Verified accounts attached to the same monitoring site can see the same live data, alert history, and deployment log for that site, including which account issued a manual deployment.
  • Legal reasons. If we are required by law, court order, or a valid request from a public authority, or if disclosure is necessary to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

We do not share personal information with any other third party for any other purpose.

7. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Account and system data are stored in Google Firebase, on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data may be processed on servers located outside the Philippines; Google maintains its own safeguards for international transfers.

We protect information with: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and Firebase; Firebase Authentication for identity; and Firebase security rules that limit each account to the data of its own barangay and block unverified accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to review our safeguards.

8. Data retention

  • Account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Alert and deployment history is kept while it is useful for monitoring and after-action review, and may be periodically cleared.
  • Sensor readings are transient operational data and are overwritten or removed on an ongoing basis.

When you delete your account, we remove your personal data as described in section 10.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate account details (you can also update your display name in the app).
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these, email omandamjerenze@gmail.com from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting. You also have the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

10. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your FloodRescueNet Monitor account and associated personal data, send an email with the subject line "Delete my account" to omandamjerenze@gmail.com from the email address registered to the account.

  • We will verify the request and delete your Firebase Authentication record and your user profile (email, display name, barangay, status and timestamps) within 30 days.
  • Deployment-log entries that record a past manual action may be retained in anonymised form (with your identity removed) where needed for the integrity of the site's incident history.
  • Backups, if any, are cycled out on a rolling basis and are not restored to active use.

11. Children's privacy

The app is a tool for adult emergency-response personnel. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Device permissions

The app requires network access (internet) to communicate with Firebase. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or SMS. If a future version needs an additional permission, this policy and the Play Store listing will be updated before that version is released.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves or as the law requires. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, provide a notice in the app or by email. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
