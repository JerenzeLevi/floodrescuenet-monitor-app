2. About the app

FloodRescueNet Monitor is a companion application for an ESP32-based automated flood response system developed as an undergraduate thesis project at Saint Columban College, College of Computing Studies, Pagadian City. The app lets authorised response personnel view water-level and system status reported by the hardware, review alert and deployment history, and issue manual deploy or retract commands to a motorized rescue-net barrier.

The app is a monitoring and research tool. It is provided for use by barangay officials and disaster response teams and is not a certified life-safety system.

The app must not be relied upon as your only means of flood detection or emergency response. Sensor readings may be affected by debris, weather, calibration, or mechanical wear.

Always maintain independent flood monitoring, established warning procedures, and direct contact with local disaster-response authorities. The rescue net is a supplementary aid and does not guarantee that any person can be safely stopped or retrieved from floodwater. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
